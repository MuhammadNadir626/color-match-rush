Color Match Rush
Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how COLOR MATCH RUSH, an offline color reflex and arcade game, handles information when you use it.

1. Information Stored on Your Device
The game may store gameplay progress and preferences locally on your device, including:

Player name
Best score and games played
Coins or in-game coin balance
Daily reward progress or claim status
Sound and other game preferences
This information helps the game remember your progress and settings between sessions.

2. Personal Information
COLOR MATCH RUSH does not require an account or ask you to provide personal information to play. Your player name is used to display your in-game profile. Please do not use sensitive personal information as your player name.

3. Offline Use and Data Sharing
The game supports offline gameplay. Information saved locally on your device, such as scores, coins, and daily reward progress, is not sent to the developer's servers.

The game does not include advertisements or use third-party analytics or online tracking services.

4. Data Security
Game information is stored on your device. Please keep your device protected, as the security of locally stored information also depends on your device and its settings.

5. Deleting Your Information
You can delete locally stored game information by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the game. This may permanently remove your saved scores, coins, daily reward progress, and preferences. Device backups may retain app data according to your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy
The game does not knowingly collect children's personal information through online services. It uses local device storage for gameplay progress and preferences.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated if the game or its privacy practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
