Color Match Rush

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Color Match Rush handles information when you use the game.

1. Information We Store

Color Match Rush currently operates offline and uses local storage on your device to save game information and preferences. This may include:

  • Player name
  • Game scores and personal bests
  • Games played
  • In-game coins and daily reward records
  • Sound and notification preferences

2. How Information Is Used

This information is used only to provide game features, save your progress, and remember your preferences.

3. Local Storage and Data Sharing

Game information is stored locally on your device. The game does not currently require an online account and does not send this locally stored game information to the developer's servers.

The game currently does not use advertising services, third-party analytics, or online tracking services.

4. Data Security

Since game information is stored locally, its security depends partly on the security of your device. Please keep your device protected.

5. Deleting Your Information

You can remove locally stored game information by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game. Please note that this may permanently delete your saved progress and preferences.

6. Children's Privacy

Color Match Rush is an offline game and does not knowingly collect children's personal information through online services. The game currently uses local device storage.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the game or its features change. Any updated version will be published on this page.
